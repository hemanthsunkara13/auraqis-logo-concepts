Brand identity · Logo & stationery exploration · Round 02

Three directions for the next AURAQIS identity.

Phase 1 keeps the marble mark you know and gives it new colour palettes and typefaces. Phase 2 explores new marks: architectural, contemporary, and made to move. Phase 3 brings them to the hand as business cards you can turn, light and inspect.

Foundation

Every concept is built from the website’s existing colours and type, so the new logo sits naturally across the site, its 3D scenes and its print materials.

Sheet 02

Phase 1 · Same mark, new voice

The original marble swirl is unchanged. Each study pairs it with a new palette and a new wordmark typeface. Use Replay to watch the reveal and Layout to switch to a horizontal lockup.

Sheet 03

Phase 2 · New marks

New marks, each built on a 120-unit construction grid and designed to animate. Pick a colourway under each mark. The size strip shows how it holds up at favicon scale.

Sheet 04

Phase 3 · In the hand

Twelve business cards built as real 3D objects, with true board thickness, painted edges, foils that catch the light, embossing and paper texture. Move your cursor to shine a lamp across a card and click to flip it. Choose Inspect in 3D to turn a card freely, rake light across its surface or examine the paper through a ×3 loupe.

Sheet 05

In context · Living with the mark

Choose any concept to see it at work: a website intro, a social profile, a business card floating above its own stack, reception signage, a site hoarding, frosted glass, stationery and app icons. Switch between day, dusk and night.
